# Authors and Co-Authors

This book is the collective knowledge record of the Riverbrand Banking Engine — written by the people who designed, built, and run the platform.

---

## Author

### Osas — Chief Technology Officer

*Author · Technical vision and system architecture*

Osas leads the engineering vision behind the Riverbrand Banking Engine: the decision to build a modular, ledger-first core around a single double-entry truth; the two-tier concurrency model that makes wallet transfers safe without sacrificing throughput; and the pluggable channel architecture that lets customers bank over WhatsApp as easily as over a mobile app. This book exists because that vision deserved a record that both engineers and executives could actually read.

## Co-Authors

### Samuel (wezard)

*Co-author · Platform engineering and documentation architecture*

Samuel (wezard) contributes the deep engineering narrative of the platform — the transaction engine, the service catalog, and the developer and operations manual — and shaped the structure of this knowledge book itself so that newly onboarded engineers can follow the money from webhook to ledger.

### Olalekan (AlicSystems)

*Co-author · Systems integration and channel engineering*

Olalekan (AlicSystems) contributes the channel and integration story: the multi-provider WhatsApp fabric, the third-party banking rails (ProvidusBank, Fincra, Flutterwave, Dojah), and the operational reality of keeping those integrations dependable when any single provider fails.

### Abednego (Geekycoder)

*Co-author · Product engineering and reliability*

Abednego (Geekycoder) contributes the reliability and product-engineering perspective — background job fleets, reconciliation, the security and session model, and the translation of those mechanisms into plain language for the non-technical track.

## About This Edition

The **Second Edition** is a full restructure of the original documentation suite. Every chapter has been rewritten against a single standard: what was built, why it was built that way, what it achieves, and where it is going. Technical and non-technical tracks are paired chapter-for-chapter so that any reader can cross the aisle in one click.
