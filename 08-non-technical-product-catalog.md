### Pillar 1: 🪪 Identity, Accounts & NUBAN Auto-Provisioning
* **Dedicated Virtual Bank Accounts (NUBAN)**: Automatically issues commercial account numbers via ProvidusBank and Fincra upon phone number verification.
* **Instant BVN & NIN Identity Verification**: Integrates directly with national identity databases (via Dojah) to confirm identity in seconds without branch visits.
* **Tiered Account Limits**: Safely grades accounts from Tier 1 (₦50k/day) to Tier 3 (₦5M+/day) with zero-panic escrow holds.

### Pillar 2: 💸 Everyday Money Movement & Bill Settlements
* **Interbank Clearing**: Instant fund transfers to any commercial bank or microfinance bank across Nigeria.
* **Zero-Fee Peer-to-Peer (P2P)**: Free transfers between Riverbrand users using phone numbers, emails, or usernames.
* **Utility Bills & Airtime Top-Up**: Direct purchases for MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile, DSTV, GOTV, Startimes, and prepaid electricity meters.
* **Smart Beneficiary Vault**: Automatically remembers saved commercial bank accounts and frequently recharged phone numbers.

### Pillar 3: 📈 Wealth Management & Compounding Savings
* **Safelock Fixed Deposits**: Capital lockup for 30, 90, 180, or 365 days with upfront yield guarantees.
* **Target Savings Goals**: Scheduled automatic deposits toward specific financial dreams (e.g. "Rent Fund", "Wedding").
* **AutoSave Sweeps**: Periodic sweeping of idle wallet balances into high-yield accounts every Friday or month-end.

### Pillar 4: 🌍 Global Multi-Currency Wallets (NGN, USD, GBP, EUR, CAD)
* **Foreign Currency Wallets**: Hold, receive, and manage funds in five major global currencies.
* **In-App Dynamic FX Conversions**: Real-time cross-currency swaps governed by administrative exchange rates.
* **Cross-Border Remittance Reception**: Direct clearing of diaspora inflows into dedicated foreign currency accounts.

### Pillar 5: 💬 24/7 Conversational WhatsApp Banking
* **Zero-App Transfers**: Complete payments and balance queries directly inside WhatsApp.
* **Interactive Button Menus & Receipts**: Instant chat receipt cards and PDF statement downloads.
* **Automated Customer Support**: 24/7 automated resolution for frequent inquiries without human teller queues.

### Pillar 6: 🏛️ Enterprise Back-Office Governance & Admin Suite
* **Executive Financial Dashboard**: Real-time visibility into total customer liabilities, active wallet reserves, and system velocity.
* **Granular Staff Permissions (RBAC)**: Ensure staff can only access tools strictly authorized for their specific job role.
* **Non-Blocking Audit Trail**: Every staff click, approval, or modification is recorded in permanent, immutable logs.
