## 🤖 8.3 Horizon 1 to Horizon 2: The Autonomous AI Personal Banker

Imagine texting your Riverbrand WhatsApp bot in everyday human language:

> *"Hey Riverbrand, I just got paid ₦1,500,000. Keep ₦400,000 for everyday spending, convert $500 to my USD Safelock, and send ₦50,000 to my sister Tolu."*

Instead of clicking through five different screens, Riverbrand's upcoming **Autonomous AI Agent** parses your intent, validates the exchange rate, checks your balance, presents a single summary confirmation card, and asks for your 4-digit PIN. One tap, and the entire multi-step financial flow executes flawlessly.
